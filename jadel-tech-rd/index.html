Engineering principles

Designed for controlled integration

Human approval

Consequential external actions can be gated behind explicit user authorization.

Least privilege

Auditability

Important actions are designed to produce durable status and evidence records.

Provider compliance

Automated actions are limited to supported APIs, account permissions and applicable platform policies.
