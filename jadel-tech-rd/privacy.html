Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026.

This policy describes how Jadel Tech RD handles information associated with this corporate website and authorized software or API-integration workflows.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the service or integration selected by an authorized user, we may process account identifiers, contact information, project or workflow data, API authorization metadata, audit records, and information voluntarily submitted through supported communication channels.

2. OAuth and third-party platforms

When a user authorizes a supported third-party platform, authentication is performed through that provider's official authorization flow where available. Jadel Tech RD does not ask users to provide third-party account passwords through this website. Access is limited to the scopes actually granted by the user and supported by the provider.

3. Purpose of processing

Information is processed to provide requested software, automation, freelance workflow management, API integration, security, troubleshooting, audit, and support functions. Data is not used to impersonate users or bypass platform security controls.

4. Human approval and automation

The AI Income Revenue Engine is designed so that consequential external actions can be subject to policy checks and explicit human approval. Availability of any write capability depends on the connected provider, granted scopes, account permissions, and applicable terms.

5. Data minimization and security

We aim to collect and retain only information needed for the selected service. Secrets such as access tokens, refresh tokens, API keys and client secrets should not be stored in public source repositories or public web pages. Where a production integration is enabled, credentials should be stored in an appropriate secret-management environment with restricted access.

6. Sharing

Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms only when required to perform a user-authorized operation through their supported interfaces. We do not sell personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Operational records may be retained as reasonably necessary for security, audit, troubleshooting, contractual or legal obligations. Users may contact us to request review or deletion of information under our control, subject to applicable retention requirements.

8. Third-party policies

Third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the policies of any platform they choose to connect.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as services, integrations or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent through the contact information on our Contact page.
